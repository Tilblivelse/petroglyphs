## Summary

Alta Museum photo archive record.

Alta has the largest collection of Rock Carvings in Northern Europe made by huntergatherers. The rock art is 2000-7000 years old and is comprised of carvings and paintings. It was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1985.
